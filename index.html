About Me

I'm an experienced and motivated backend developer from Bagan, Myanmar, with 3+ years of professional expertise in Laravel and PHP. Since January 2023 I've been building and maintaining ConceptX — a Learning Management & School Management platform — where I design database structures, develop RESTful APIs, lead feature planning, mentor junior developers, and run the full testing cycle from unit tests to end-to-end regression testing. I'm a deep thinker with strong problem-solving skills — I can independently design an entire project flow from business requirements, and database flow design is one of my greatest strengths.

I hold the NCC Education Level 4 and Level 5 Diplomas in Computing, earning Distinctions in Agile Development, Data Analysis & Visualisation with Python, and Network Security & Cryptography. Alongside my development work, I also teach computing to Primary 4, 5 and 6 students.
