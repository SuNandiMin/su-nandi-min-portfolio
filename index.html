About Me

I'm a Backend Software Engineer with 3+ years of professional experience building and maintaining production applications with PHP, Laravel, MySQL and REST APIs. Since January 2023 I've worked at ConceptX on EdOmnis, a production Learning Management and School Management platform. There I design database structures, build and optimize REST APIs for web and mobile clients, and implement authentication, roles and policy-based permissions. I test features at every level, from unit tests to end-to-end regression.

Beyond writing code, I take part in feature planning, mentor junior developers, support deployments and production issues, and write documentation and train users. My strength is turning business requirements into a clear system design (the data model, API contract and permission rules) before implementation starts.

I hold the NCC Education Level 4 and Level 5 Diplomas in Computing, with Distinctions in Agile Development, Data Analysis & Visualisation with Python, and Network Security & Cryptography. I also teach computing to Primary 4–6 students.
